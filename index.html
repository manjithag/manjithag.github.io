About Me

I am a Research Associate at the Deggendorf Institute of Technology, Germany, where I conduct in-depth research on data privacy, anonymization, open data systems and their applications in AI. My current work focuses on developing privacy risk audit systems that integrate advanced AI agents with mathematical algorithms for comprehensive data evaluation.

With a strong background in electrical engineering and Information Technology, I bridge the gap between theoretical research and practical implementation of these disciplines in the rapidly evolving field of AI and data privacy.
