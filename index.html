About Me

I am a Research Associate at the Deggendorf Institute of Technology, Germany, where I conduct in-depth research on AI applications in data privacy, anonymization, and open data systems. My work focuses on developing privacy risk audit systems that integrate advanced AI agents with mathematical algorithms for comprehensive data evaluation.

With a strong background in electrical engineering and a Master's degree in Electrical Engineering and Information Technology, I bridge the gap between theoretical research and practical implementation in the rapidly evolving field of AI and data privacy.
